LAGOON 1.0 - a notch and floating dock for Windows 11
=====================================================

INSTALL
- Run LagoonSetup-1.0.0.exe. No admin rights needed; nothing else to install.
- Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't signed.
  Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
- To update later, just run a newer setup: it closes Lagoon and replaces it.

USING IT
- A small notch sits at the top of the screen. Hover or tap it: music, clipboard history,
  widgets (clock, weather, to-dos, battery), timer and stopwatch, quick controls.
- Long-press the notch for a quick peek at battery, RAM and CPU.
- Right-click the notch > Settings to turn on:
  - Floating dock (replaces the taskbar; the Windows taskbar comes back when you turn it off or quit)
  - Notifications through the notch (instead of Windows' pop-ups)
  - Start with Windows
- Ctrl+Alt+M mutes/unmutes your microphone everywhere.
- Search "Lagoon" in Start to open it again; if it's already running the notch says so.

QUIT / UNINSTALL
- Quit: right-click the notch > Quit Lagoon. Everything it changed (taskbar, notification
  pop-ups) is put back.
- Uninstall: Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Lagoon > Uninstall.
- Your settings live in %APPDATA%\Lagoon (kept after uninstall; delete the folder to reset).

Needs Windows 11 (or Windows 10 version 2004+), 64-bit.
